Welcome, {first_name}. You're in.

Chicago businesses give creators like you 2 free tickets to their events, one for you and one for a friend. All they ask for is an honest video. Here's how it works:

  1. 1Claim. When an event opens up to you, tap Claim tickets. You'll get a code for 2 free tickets.
  2. 2Grab your tickets. Use the code on the business's ticket site, then tap "I got my tickets" in the app.
  3. 3Go. Bring a friend and have fun.
  4. 4Post. Within 48 hours, share an honest video on TikTok or Instagram, tag the business, and add #localcurrentapp.

One thing to know: bigger accounts see each event first, and every 24 hours it opens to the next group. Turn on notifications so you catch yours.
